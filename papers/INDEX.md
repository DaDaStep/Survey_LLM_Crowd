## Status legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `candidate` | Identified by search, not yet screened. |
| `in-scope` | Passes the inclusion criteria; full note required. |
| `out-of-scope` | Excluded; brief reason recorded. |
| `duplicate` | Already represented by another entry; pointer to canonical. |
